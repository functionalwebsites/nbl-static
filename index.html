Newport Barcode & Label

A simpler way to buy barcode supplies.

Ordering barcode supplies online can go wrong fast when core size, outside diameter, print technology, ribbon formula, adhesive, or printer compatibility is missed. Newport Barcode & Label should use the website as a clean intake path, then close orders by phone when the details matter.

That call-first model is a better idea than forcing every product into static product pages. It protects customers from buying incompatible supplies and keeps the site easier to maintain while PDF catalogs and inventory lists are still being assembled.
